The tin pod

Lift, swing, click shut.

The pod sits on the same axis as the can, so the caddie stays one clean cylinder. The door opens away from the cart, so you never lift the caddie off the frame.

  1. Lift the tab

    The magnet lets go with a light pull on the leather latch tab.

  2. Swing the door

    The curved door pivots on a steel pin and the tin slides out onto your palm.

  3. Push it closed

    Nudge the door back and the magnet pulls it shut against the steel strike.
